# Sales Data Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

A company needs to understand its sales performance, identify its most profitable products, and detect trends that can influence business growth.

Without proper data analysis, it is difficult to identify sales opportunities, monitor performance, and make informed commercial decisions.

## 2. Project Objective

Analyze sales data to evaluate business performance, identify revenue drivers, and generate actionable insights that support better decision-making.

## 3. Data Analysis Workflow

- **Data Collection:** Import the sales dataset.
- **Data Exploration:** Examine the dataset structure, variables, and statistical distributions.
- **Data Cleaning:** Handle missing values, duplicates, inconsistent formats, and incorrect data.
- **Data Transformation:** Prepare and derive relevant analytical variables.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Analyze sales trends, product performance, and revenue distribution.
- **Data Visualization:** Build clear visualizations to communicate key findings.

## 4. Key Insights

- Overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Best-performing and least-performing products.
- Sales distribution across categories.
- Revenue evolution over time.
- Potential patterns and business opportunities.

## 5. Business Decisions

The analysis helps businesses identify their strongest products, optimize sales strategies, improve inventory planning, and prioritize profitable opportunities.

## 6. Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Microsoft Excel

## 7. Project Outcome

A structured sales analysis that transforms raw data into meaningful insights and supports data-driven commercial decisions.

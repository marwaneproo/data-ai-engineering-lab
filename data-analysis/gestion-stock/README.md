# Inventory Management Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

Businesses need to maintain an appropriate inventory level to satisfy customer demand while avoiding stock shortages and unnecessary storage costs.

Poor inventory visibility can lead to operational inefficiencies, lost sales, and overstocking.

## 2. Project Objective

Analyze inventory data to evaluate stock levels, identify potential shortages, monitor product availability, and support better inventory management.

## 3. Data Analysis Workflow

- **Data Collection:** Import the inventory dataset.
- **Data Exploration:** Examine products, quantities, categories, and stock indicators.
- **Data Cleaning:** Handle missing values, duplicates, inconsistent records, and invalid quantities.
- **Data Transformation:** Calculate stock indicators and organize inventory information.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Analyze stock distribution, product availability, and inventory levels.
- **Data Visualization:** Create stock monitoring charts and category comparisons.

## 4. Key Insights

- Total inventory and stock distribution.
- Products with critical stock levels.
- Overstocked and understocked products.
- Inventory distribution across categories.
- Products requiring replenishment.

## 5. Business Decisions

The analysis helps businesses prioritize replenishment, reduce stockout risks, optimize inventory levels, and improve operational planning.

## 7. Project Outcome

An inventory analysis that transforms stock records into actionable operational insights and supports more efficient inventory management.
